Key Advantages

Unlike many international conference destinations, Lisbon offers a rare advantage: the airport is located within the city itself. This allows participants to reach the venue in under 15 minutes, significantly reducing travel fatigue and logistical complexity.

Fast, simple, and stress-free arrival for all attendees.

Strategic Advantage for EME 2028

The proximity between airport and venue ensures maximum attendance efficiency, minimizes transfer risks, and enhances the overall conference experience. Participants can arrive and be at the venue in a matter of minutes — a key factor for international events.
