The organizing committee of the EME2028 Conference invites proposals to host the EME2030 Conference. This is a unique opportunity to bring together the global Earth–Moon–Earth (EME) community in your region.

Submission deadline: [INSERT DATE]
Selected candidates may be invited to present their proposal during EME2028.

📍 Proposal Requirements

Proposals should include key information about the proposed location, venue, organizing team, and estimated costs. A detailed proposal document (PDF recommended) is strongly encouraged.

🏢 Venue & Facilities

  • Capacity for approximately 100–150 participants
  • Conference rooms and presentation facilities
  • Audio/visual equipment and internet connectivity
  • On-site or nearby accommodation options

✈️ Accessibility

  • Proximity to an international airport
  • Convenient transportation options
  • Ease of travel for international attendees

👥 Organizing Team

  • Experienced and committed local team
  • Ability to manage logistics and coordination

💰 Budget & Sponsorship

  • Estimated participant costs
  • Accommodation pricing
  • Potential sponsorship and institutional support

⭐ Evaluation Criteria

  • Accessibility and travel convenience
  • Cost-effectiveness
  • Quality of venue and facilities
  • Strength of organizing team
  • Overall attractiveness of the destination

📎 How to Apply

Complete the online submission form and provide as much detail as possible. You may also include a link or upload a detailed proposal document.
