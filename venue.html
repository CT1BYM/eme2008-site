Capacity & Suitability

The venue comfortably accommodates 150 participants in a main conference room, with additional breakout areas for parallel sessions, exhibitions, and networking. All facilities are located within the same building, ensuring a seamless experience.

Why This Venue Wins

The Altis Grand Hotel provides a rare combination of central location, premium quality, and cost efficiency. By hosting accommodation and conference in the same venue, logistical complexity is minimized while maximizing participant comfort and engagement.
