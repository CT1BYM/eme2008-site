Why Lisbon

About the Conference

The EME 2028 Conference will bring together leading experts in Earth–Moon–Earth communication, advanced microwave systems, and radio science. Hosted in Lisbon, the event combines cutting-edge technical sessions with networking and cultural experiences.

Why Lisbon

Global Accessibility

Lisbon offers direct connections to Europe, North America, and beyond, with a major international airport just minutes from the city center.

Proven Conference Infrastructure

Modern conference hotels, advanced technical facilities, and experienced event hosting ensure a seamless experience.

Safe & Welcoming Environment

Portugal is one of the safest countries in the world, known for hospitality, excellent food, and a vibrant atmosphere.

Strong EME Community

Local organizers are actively engaged in cutting-edge EME research, including high-frequency (47 GHz and 76GHz) lunar reflection experiments.
