Experiences Beyond the Conference

Optional guided tours for accompanying guests

Designed for Accompanying Guests

While participants engage in the technical program, accompanying guests are invited to enjoy a curated selection of experiences across Lisbon and its surroundings.

These guided tours are designed to provide comfort, culture, and memorable moments — making the EME 2028 conference a complete experience for everyone.

Sintra & Pena Palace

UNESCO World Heritage site with stunning palaces and breathtaking scenery.

Perfect for a relaxed cultural day during conference sessions.

Cascais & Guincho

Charming coastal town with beautiful beaches and Atlantic views.

A scenic and refreshing escape by the ocean.

São Jorge Castle

Historic fortress offering panoramic views over Lisbon.

A gentle walk through history in the heart of the city.

Faraday Museum (IST)

A unique visit connecting science, history, and innovation.

Ideal for guests with curiosity for science and technology.

Belém District

Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower — icons of Portuguese discoveries.

A cultural highlight combined with local gastronomy.

Alfama District

The oldest neighborhood in Lisbon, rich in history and Fado music.

Authentic atmosphere and unforgettable charm.

More Than a Conference

EME 2028 is designed as a shared experience.

We understand that many participants travel with partners, and Lisbon offers the perfect setting to combine science, culture, and lifestyle.

A conference where both participants and their companions create unforgettable memories.
